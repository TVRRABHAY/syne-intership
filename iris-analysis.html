The Science

What is Iris Analysis?

Iris Analysis (also known as Iridology) is the scientific study of the iris — the colored part of the eye. Each iris contains unique patterns, textures, and fiber structures that are directly connected to the brain through the optic nerve.

The human iris has over 200 unique identification points (more than a fingerprint's 35), making it one of the most complex external structures in the human body. Our AI technology maps these patterns to provide detailed insights into cognitive abilities, personality traits, and innate intelligence.
